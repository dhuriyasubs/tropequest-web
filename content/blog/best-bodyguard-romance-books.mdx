He&apos;s paid to protect her. He is not paid to fall in love with her. Bodyguard romance puts two people in each other&apos;s space around the clock, one of them bound by professional rules the other keeps quietly testing, and lets the tension build in every hallway, every car ride, every night shift. Here are five books that make the most of it.

## 1. [The Road Of Bones](/books/the-road-of-bones-demi-winters-mo452kku-gkcyz) by Demi Winters

<BookCover slug="the-road-of-bones-demi-winters-mo452kku-gkcyz" />

Silla Nordvig is running for her life when her father&apos;s dying words send her on a perilous journey with a crew of Viking mercenaries as her only protection. A forbidden romance builds inside the danger, with a secret threatening to unravel everything by the end.

**Why it works:** Swapping a single bodyguard for a whole crew of protectors raises the stakes and multiplies the tension without losing the core dynamic.

## 2. [This Damsel Is Not in Distress](/books/this-damsel-is-not-in-distress-tee-harlowe) by Tee Harlowe

<BookCover slug="this-damsel-is-not-in-distress-tee-harlowe" />

Niamh Merridon flees her war-torn kingdom expecting to find safety alone, and instead gets stuck with Rafe &quot;the Wolfe&quot; Wolfgang, a grumpy guard who is completely unmoved by her charm, and who happens to know she&apos;s betrothed to his prince.

**Why it works:** A bodyguard who refuses to be charmed is a much better opponent than one who falls too easily, and the slow crack in his resistance is the whole payoff.

## 3. [Dare](/books/dare-heather-long) by Heather Long

<BookCover slug="dare-heather-long" />

A woman still hunting for her sister and still tearing down the empire that stole her, protected by a group of men who have become her protectors, her lovers, and her family all at once. Dark, high-stakes, and unflinching about what survival costs.

**Why it works:** Multiple protectors sharing the role means the devotion never has to be split, it just compounds.

## 4. [Girl Gone Viral](/books/girl-gone-viral) by Alisha Rai

<BookCover slug="girl-gone-viral" />

An accidental viral celebrity suddenly needs real protection, and the bodyguard assigned to keep her safe has to do his job while dealing with the fact that keeping his distance isn&apos;t as easy as it sounds.

**Why it works:** The contemporary, internet-era setup gives an old trope a genuinely modern reason for the danger to feel real.

## 5. [Protecting What&apos;s His](/books/protecting-whats-his) by Tessa Bailey

<BookCover slug="protecting-whats-his" />

A steamy romantic suspense built around a protective hero who will stop at absolutely nothing to keep the woman he loves safe, once he&apos;s decided she&apos;s his to protect.

**Why it works:** Bailey writes possessive heroes better than almost anyone, and the bodyguard setup gives that intensity a built-in professional excuse.
