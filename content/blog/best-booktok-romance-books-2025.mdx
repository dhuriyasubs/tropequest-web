BookTok does not slow down. Every few months there&apos;s a new obsession, a new series everyone is reading simultaneously, a new author the algorithm decided to make unavoidable. Here is what 2025 looks like.

## 1. [Onyx Storm](/books/onyx-storm) by Rebecca Yarros

<BookCover slug="onyx-storm" />

The third book in the Empyrean series. Violet Sorrengail and Xaden Riorson return, and nothing is as it was. The fandom collectively lost its mind when this came out in January 2025.

**Why it&apos;s everywhere:** The Empyrean series is the biggest thing to happen to romantasy in years. If you haven&apos;t started with Fourth Wing yet, now is the time.

## 2. [Fourth Wing](/books/fourth-wing) by Rebecca Yarros

<BookCover slug="fourth-wing" />

War College. Dragons. A heroine who was never supposed to survive first year. Xaden Riorson. If you have managed to avoid this book until now, consider your avoidance officially over.

**Why it&apos;s everywhere:** This is the book that made romantasy a household word. It has been on BookTok since 2023 and it is not going anywhere.

## 3. [Things We Never Got Over](/books/things-we-never-got-over) by Lucy Score

<BookCover slug="things-we-never-got-over" />

Naomi arrives in a small town with nothing. Knox Morgan, the grumpy local mechanic, wants nothing to do with her. The small-town setting, the grumpy hero, and the genuinely sweet payoff made this one of the most recommended books of the past two years.

**Why it&apos;s everywhere:** Grumpy/sunshine in a small town with a slow burn. The algorithm loves it and readers do too.

## 4. [Haunting Adeline](/books/haunting-adeline) by H.D. Carlton

<BookCover slug="haunting-adeline" />

The stalker romance that refuses to leave BookTok. Dark, deeply unhinged, and somehow impossible to put down. Not for everyone. On everyone&apos;s For You page.

**Why it&apos;s everywhere:** Dark romance is having a multi-year moment, and this is the book at the centre of it.

## 5. [Twisted Love](/books/twisted-love) by Ana Huang

<BookCover slug="twisted-love" />

Alex Volkov is cold, calculating, and has been secretly watching over his best friend&apos;s sister for years. When they&apos;re finally forced together, the tension is immediate and consuming.

**Why it&apos;s everywhere:** The Twisted series is the gateway drug for an entire generation of dark romance readers. Twisted Love is always the recommended starting point.

## 6. [Icebreaker](/books/icebreaker) by Hannah Grace

<BookCover slug="icebreaker" />

A figure skater and a hockey captain forced to share ice time. The sports romance genre has exploded on BookTok and this is consistently the most recommended title.

**Why it&apos;s everywhere:** Sports romance has its own corner of BookTok now, and Icebreaker is the book everyone points to first.

## 7. [From Blood and Ash](/books/from-blood-and-ash) by Jennifer L. Armentrout

<BookCover slug="from-blood-and-ash" />

Poppy has lived her whole life under strict rules she doesn&apos;t understand. Hawke is her guard, and he is not what he seems. Fantasy romance with real stakes and a plot twist that will genuinely break something in you.

**Why it&apos;s everywhere:** The Blood and Ash series has one of the most dedicated fandoms in romance. Every new reader is immediately sent to book one.

## 8. [Funny Story](/books/funny-story) by Emily Henry

<BookCover slug="funny-story" />

Daphne and Miles have nothing in common except that their exes just got together. So they agree to fake date to save face. Emily Henry at her most warm and quietly devastating.

**Why it&apos;s everywhere:** Emily Henry is BookTok royalty. Everything she releases becomes required reading within days.

## 9. [Just for the Summer](/books/just-for-the-summer) by Abby Jimenez

<BookCover slug="just-for-the-summer" />

Two people with a history of bad luck in love make a pact: date each other for the summer, break up, and use the curse to finally find the right person. Except the plan starts falling apart immediately.

**Why it&apos;s everywhere:** Abby Jimenez has built one of the most loyal followings on BookTok. This is her most recent hit and it lived on the algorithm for months.

## 10. [A Court of Thorns and Roses](/books/a-court-of-thorns-and-roses) by Sarah J. Maas

<BookCover slug="a-court-of-thorns-and-roses" />

A huntress is taken to a magical land after killing a wolf in the forest. What follows is a series that reshaped the entire fantasy romance genre. It never leaves BookTok because someone new is always discovering it.

**Why it&apos;s everywhere:** This series is the reason the word romantasy exists. It has been recommended continuously since 2015 and the momentum has not stopped.
