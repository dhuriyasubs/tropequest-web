Most readers have two reading lives. The books on their shelves at home, and the books on their Goodreads or StoryGraph profile. These two lists rarely match, because adding books one by one is a chore most people never finish.

TropeQuest has a faster way. One photo, and your entire shelf is imported automatically.

## How to scan your bookshelf

### Step 1: Go to My Shelf

[Open My Shelf →](/shelf) and sign in if prompted. At the top of the page you will see a small camera icon next to the search bar. Tap it.

![My Shelf page showing the camera icon at the top](/images/blog/shelf-scan/step-1-shelf-camera-icon.png)

---

### Step 2: Take a photo or upload one

Point your phone camera at a shelf and tap the shutter, or upload a photo you have already taken. You can scan a single shelf, a row of books, or an entire bookcase wall.

![Camera screen showing a bookshelf being photographed](/images/blog/shelf-scan/step-2-take-photo.png)

A few tips for the best results:
- Make sure the spines are facing forward and reasonably well lit
- Angled or very worn spines may not scan cleanly — add those manually afterwards
- You can scan in multiple rounds; books are not duplicated

---

### Step 3: Review the detected books

TropeQuest reads the spines and shows you a list of everything it identified. Each detected book appears with its cover, title, and author so you can verify it quickly.

![Scan results page showing detected books with covers and titles](/images/blog/shelf-scan/step-3-scan-results.png)

Check each result:
- **Correct** — leave it ticked
- **Wrong match** — untick it and use the search bar to find the right book
- **Missed entirely** — use the search bar on the same page to add it manually

---

### Step 4: Add to your library

Once you are happy with the list, tap **Add to Shelf**. All confirmed books are added to your library in one go with a status of **Unread**.

![Confirmation screen after adding scanned books to shelf](/images/blog/shelf-scan/step-4-add-to-shelf.png)

That is it. No typing, no searching one by one, no getting distracted halfway through.

---

## What you get after the scan

Your shelf now shows the total count of books you own, broken down by status: **Unread**, **Reading**, and **Read**. Update a status any time by tapping the book.

![Shelf overview showing book count and status breakdown](/images/blog/shelf-scan/step-5-shelf-overview.png)

Your shelf is fully searchable and filterable by genre, status, or title. And because every book in TropeQuest is tagged with tropes, your scanned books connect directly to the discovery features — you can find what is similar to books you already own, see the next book in a series, or explore by the tropes you love most.

## If a book is not recognised

Occasionally a spine is angled, worn, or too small to read clearly. If a book does not get identified, the search bar on the same page lets you add it manually in seconds.

There is also a feedback button on scan results. If TropeQuest misidentifies a book, flagging it helps improve future scans.
