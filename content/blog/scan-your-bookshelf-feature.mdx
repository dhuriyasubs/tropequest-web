Most readers have two reading lives. The books on their shelves at home, and the books on their Goodreads or StoryGraph profile. These two lists rarely match, because adding books one by one is a chore most people never finish.

TropeQuest has a faster way. One photo, and your entire shelf is imported automatically.

## How to scan your bookshelf

<ShelfScanScreenshots />

**Step 1 — Open My Shelf and tap the camera.** Go to [My Shelf →](/shelf) and sign in if prompted. Tap the pink camera button in the top right, or the **Scan your shelf** button on an empty shelf. Choose **Take photo** or **Choose from gallery**.

**Step 2 — Take a photo and crop.** Point your phone at a shelf and take the photo. TropeQuest shows a **Crop &amp; Scan** screen where you can drag to select a section, or tap **Scan full image** to process everything at once.

**Step 3 — Review the detected books.** TropeQuest reads the spines and shows a **Books Found** list with title, author, and cover for each one it identified. Every book is ticked by default. Tap **Wrong?** next to any misidentified book to swap it, or **+ Scan section** to scan more of the shelf. When ready, tap **Add N books to Shelf**.

**Step 4 — Your shelf is ready.** All confirmed books land instantly, grouped by genre, each with a status of **Unread**. Filter by All, Unread, Reading, or Read at the top.

---

## What you get after the scan

Your shelf is fully searchable and filterable by genre, status, or title. Update any book&apos;s status to **Reading** or **Read** as you go.

Because every book in TropeQuest is tagged with tropes, your scanned books connect directly to the discovery features — find what is similar to books you already own, see the next book in a series, or explore by the tropes you love most.

## If a book is not recognised

Occasionally a spine is angled, worn, or too small to read clearly. The search bar on the shelf page lets you add it manually in seconds. Tap **+ Scan section** on the results screen to scan another part of the shelf without starting over.
