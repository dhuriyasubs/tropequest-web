Most readers have two reading lives. The books on their shelves at home, and the books on their Goodreads or StoryGraph profile. These two lists rarely match, because adding books one by one is a chore most people never finish.

TropeQuest has a faster way. One photo, and your entire shelf is imported automatically.

## How to scan your bookshelf

### Step 1: Open My Shelf and tap the camera

Go to [My Shelf →](/shelf) and sign in if prompted. Tap the pink camera button in the top right corner, or the **Scan your shelf** button on an empty shelf. A menu appears with two options: **Take photo** or **Choose from gallery**.

![My Shelf page with Scan your shelf button and camera options](/images/blog/shelf-scan/step-1-my-shelf.png)

---

### Step 2: Take or confirm the photo

Point your phone at a shelf and take the photo. TropeQuest will show you a preview of the image with a ✓ button to confirm and an ✗ button to retake. Make sure the book spines are facing forward and reasonably well lit, then confirm.

![Bookshelf photo preview with confirm and retake buttons](/images/blog/shelf-scan/step-2-photo-preview.png)

---

### Step 3: Crop and scan

A **Crop &amp; Scan** screen appears showing your photo. You can drag to select just a section of the shelf, or tap **Scan full image** to process everything at once.

![Crop and Scan screen with the option to select a section or scan the full image](/images/blog/shelf-scan/step-3-crop-scan.png)

---

### Step 4: Review the detected books

TropeQuest reads the spines and shows you a **Books Found** list — title, author, and cover for each one it identified. Every book is ticked by default.

![Books Found screen showing 10 identified books with checkboxes and Add to Shelf button](/images/blog/shelf-scan/step-4-books-found.png)

Go through the list:
- **Correct** — leave it ticked
- **Wrong match** — tap **Wrong?** next to it and search for the right book
- **Missed entirely** — tap **+ Scan section** to scan another part of the shelf, or add it manually via search

When you are happy, tap **Add N books to Shelf**.

---

### Step 5: Your shelf is ready

All confirmed books are added instantly, grouped by genre, each with a status of **Unread**. You can see the total count and filter by All, Unread, Reading, or Read.

![My Shelf showing 10 books added, grouped by genre with status filters](/images/blog/shelf-scan/step-5-shelf-result.png)

That is it. No typing, no searching title by title, no getting distracted halfway through.

---

## What you get after the scan

Your shelf is fully searchable and filterable by genre, status, or title. Update any book&apos;s status to **Reading** or **Read** as you go.

Because every book in TropeQuest is tagged with tropes, your scanned books connect directly to the discovery features — find what is similar to books you already own, see the next book in a series, or explore by the tropes you love most.

## If a book is not recognised

Occasionally a spine is angled, worn, or too small to read clearly. The search bar on the same shelf page lets you add it manually in seconds. You can also tap **+ Scan section** on the results screen to scan another part of the shelf without starting over.
