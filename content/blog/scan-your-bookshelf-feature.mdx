Most readers have two reading lives. The books on their shelves at home, and the books on their Goodreads or StoryGraph profile. These two lists rarely match, because adding books one by one is a chore most people never finish.

TropeQuest has a faster way.

## Take a photo, get your shelf

On the My Shelf page, tap the camera icon. Point your phone at any bookshelf, a single shelf or the whole wall, and take a photo.

TropeQuest scans the image, reads the spines, and identifies the books. Within seconds you get a list of everything it found. Confirm the ones it got right, skip any it missed, and they are added to your library in one go.

No typing. No searching for each title individually. No getting distracted halfway through.

## What happens after the scan

Every book that gets added lands in your shelf with a status of **Unread** by default. From there you can mark any book as **Reading** or **Read** as you go.

Your shelf is then fully searchable and filterable. You can browse by genre, by status, or search for a specific title. And because every book in the TropeQuest catalogue is tagged with tropes, your scanned books connect directly to the discovery features: you can see what is similar to books you already own, find the next book in a series, or explore by the tropes you love most.

## If a book is not recognised

Occasionally a spine is angled, worn, or too small to read clearly. If a book does not get identified, you can add it manually with the search bar on the same page.

There is also a feedback button on scan results. If TropeQuest misidentifies a book, flagging it helps improve future scans.
