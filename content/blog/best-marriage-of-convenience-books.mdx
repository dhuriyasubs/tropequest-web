It starts as a transaction. Money, protection, reputation, a business deal that needs a ring attached to it. Two people agree to marry for reasons that have nothing to do with love, set their rules, and keep their distance. Marriage of convenience works because forced proximity is already built in: two people sharing a life while pretending not to feel anything, until the rules stop holding.

Here are 10 books that get the slow unraveling exactly right.

## 1. [Forever After All](/books/forever-after-all-catharina-maura) by Catharina Maura

<BookCover slug="forever-after-all-catharina-maura" />

Elena walks into a gentlemen&apos;s club ready to sell her body to save her mother&apos;s life, and walks out with a marriage proposal instead. Alexander Kennedy offers a marriage of convenience with strings attached: she&apos;s about to become a tool in his revenge plan, whether she realizes it yet or not.

**Why it works:** The power imbalance between them is the entire engine of the tension, and Maura never lets you forget the stakes on either side.

## 2. [The Devious Husband](/books/the-devious-husband) by Catharina Maura

<BookCover slug="the-devious-husband" />

Xavier Kingston can&apos;t stand his business rival Sierra Windsor, or so he tells himself, right up until she starts showing interest in someone who isn&apos;t him. An enemies-to-lovers arranged marriage where one half of the couple has been quietly in love the whole time.

**Why it works:** The dramatic irony of a one-sided secret inside a marriage neither of them chose gives every scene a second layer.

## 3. [Pucking Strong](/books/pucking-strong-emily-rath) by Emily Rath

<BookCover slug="pucking-strong-emily-rath" />

A pro hockey team&apos;s Swedish star forward and the team&apos;s physical therapist stage a very real fake marriage to solve a very real problem, in a spicy, slow-burn MM workplace romance from a viral breakout author.

**Why it works:** Sports romance gives the fake-marriage premise a built-in audience of witnesses, which makes keeping up the act constantly, deliciously stressful.

## 4. [Dawn of the North](/books/dawn-of-the-north-demi-winters) by Demi Winters

<BookCover slug="dawn-of-the-north-demi-winters" />

The third book in the Viking-inspired Ashen romantasy series, where a returned heir has to earn the loyalty of the northern jarls with the warrior who&apos;s captured her heart standing beside her, bound together by duty as much as by choice.

**Why it works:** Romantasy raises the stakes of the marriage-of-convenience setup from social consequence to genuinely political ones.

## 5. [Eyes of Silver, Eyes of Gold](/books/eyes-of-silver-eyes-of-gold-ellen-o-connell) by Ellen O&apos;Connell

<BookCover slug="eyes-of-silver-eyes-of-gold-ellen-o-connell" />

Set in Colorado in 1885, this is the story of a family scandal, a spinster with a habit of embarrassing her rigidly proper family, and a marriage that starts as damage control and becomes something neither of them expected.

**Why it works:** The western setting adds real, physical stakes to a trope that&apos;s usually confined to drawing rooms.

## 6. [Devil in Winter](/books/devil-in-winter-wallflowers-3) by Lisa Kleypas

<BookCover slug="devil-in-winter-wallflowers-3" />

A wallflower with nothing left to lose proposes marriage to a notorious rake who needs money as badly as she needs an escape. A genre-defining Regency marriage of convenience from one of historical romance&apos;s most influential authors.

**Why it works:** Kleypas built the modern template for this exact setup, and this is the book most readers point to first.

## 7. [A Substitute Wife for the Prizefighter](/books/a-substitute-wife-for-the-prizefighter-alice-coldbreath) by Alice Coldbreath

<BookCover slug="a-substitute-wife-for-the-prizefighter-alice-coldbreath" />

Lizzie Anderson&apos;s family is torn apart by scandal and she&apos;s thrown out onto the street with nowhere to go, until an unlikely marriage offer changes everything. Part of Coldbreath&apos;s hugely popular Prizefighter series, built entirely around practical marriages that turn into real ones.

**Why it works:** Lizzie&apos;s steady, principled voice grounds a premise that could easily tip into melodrama.

## 8. [Princess of the Void](/books/princess-of-the-void-an-alien-abduction-romance-vols-15-complete-vol-6-begins-in-may) by Dukerino

<BookCover slug="princess-of-the-void-an-alien-abduction-romance-vols-15-complete-vol-6-begins-in-may" />

A guard assigned to watch a captured alien prisoner ends up bound to her by circumstances neither of them chose, in a genre-bending sci-fi romance that leans hard into forced proximity. One of Royal Road&apos;s most-read romances for a reason.

**Why it works:** Taking the trope out of ballrooms and into a sci-fi setting makes the stakes feel genuinely unpredictable.

## 9. [The Unexpected Engagement of the Marvelous Mr. Penn](/books/the-unexpected-engagement-of-the-marvelous-mr-penn) by Rkgoff

<BookCover slug="the-unexpected-engagement-of-the-marvelous-mr-penn" />

A hundred and fifty wedding invitations go out across the city for the marriage of Lady Eleanor Serrs to Mr. Ryce Penn, and the newspapers can&apos;t stop buzzing about it. A Victorian-set web original with a heist-adjacent streak running underneath the romance.

**Why it works:** The public spectacle of the engagement raises the cost of anything going wrong, which keeps every chapter tense.

## 10. [Nobody&apos;s Baby But Mine](/books/nobodys-baby-but-mine-chicago-stars-3) by Susan Elizabeth Phillips

<BookCover slug="nobodys-baby-but-mine-chicago-stars-3" />

A brilliant, exacting woman and a football star with more charm than sense end up bound together for the sake of a baby neither of them planned for. A beloved contemporary classic that helped define the sports-romance-meets-marriage-of-convenience mashup.

**Why it works:** The mismatch between the two leads makes their eventual truce, and everything after it, feel earned.
